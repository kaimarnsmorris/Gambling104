basis_alt

Expected: The 15 s basis tracker instead of the shipped 60 s one. NOTES E19 found 15 s is the constrained optimum of the extended sweep, and the shipped 60 s was a boundary optimum, so this is the open question that a live comparison was meant to settle. Selecting the alt tracker selects its level AND its residual together (b_alt paired with eps_alt = d_lvl - b_alt); pairing one tracker's level with the other's residual would double-count. Expected to move the LEVEL channel only: eps_bar, and p_ref/s/sigma because p_ref = exp(x_t + b_t) carries the tracked level as a scale factor. Measured, carry, var_eps, var_basis and m_Y are all bit-for-bit identical to baseline and Var_Y itself agrees to 1e-15 relative - an earlier draft of this note claimed the carry and var_eps move, and they do not: the Chainlink carry is built from win.F and x_t, which carry no basis, and the eps variance is q_unit * sigma_at(v_loc)^2, which never sees the tracker.
Observed: near-strike last-30 s log-loss -0.03116, pooled -0.00147 against baseline. Variance level by remaining time: {"1-5": 0.236, "6-15": 0.284, "16-30": 0.409, "31-60": 0.553, "61-120": 0.69, "121-300": 0.914}.

Finding: FIX-ROUND 2, corrected: an earlier version of this finding, and of the variant's own note, claimed basis_alt moves 'eps_bar and the carry' and also var_eps. Measured on this export, only the LEVEL channel moves: eps_bar (max |delta| 6.6e-05), and p_ref / s / sigma because p_ref = exp(x_t + b_t) carries the tracked level as a scale factor. carry, var_eps, var_basis and m_Y are ALL bit-for-bit identical to baseline, and Var_Y itself agrees to 1.1e-15 relative. The note in variants/basis_alt.yaml has been corrected to match. The variant itself performs well - second-best near30 of the 16 (-0.0312) and one of five that improve pooled log-loss (-0.0015).
